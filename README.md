# Agency OS: 19-Day TikTok Shop Client Sprint

This is the operating plan for our four-founder agency in Karachi. **Goal: 4 new TikTok Shop Management clients in 19 days** (Thu 5 Nov to Mon 23 Nov 2026), then keep going to the 60-day plan.

The math says 4 clients in 19 days is possible if we hit the numbers. Working backwards from 4 closes, we need about **45 meetings booked**, which means about **300 dials a day, 200+ cold emails a day once inboxes are ready, and 60 manual DMs a day**. The plan below is built around those numbers.

## Fix these before Day 1

1. **Check how the first client's orders are fulfilled, today.** Our current model buys products on Amazon and ships them to TikTok Shop buyers. Recent reporting says TikTok Shop's US **Order Packaging Requirements (published September 2026) ban dropshipping directly from other retailers to customers**, and TikTok's guidance says packages must not carry another marketplace's invoice, packaging or branding. If the shop gets suspended we lose the client *and* our only case study. Huraiz should read the current rules in Seller Center (Seller University / policy center) and move the shop to compliant fulfillment: the client's own stock, a real wholesale or dropship supplier with neutral packaging, or **Amazon MCF from the seller's own FBA stock** (Amazon supports this for TikTok Shop through approved connector apps). See [plan/04](plan/04-icp-markets-lead-generation.md#why-amazon-fba-brands-are-our-best-target).
2. **The operator budget (PKR 10k to 15k) is below Sindh's legal minimum wage for a full-time job.** The minimum is PKR 40,000/month for unskilled workers (FY 2025-26, and reportedly PKR 43,000 from July 2026). So hire **part-time** at that budget and build in a step-up to full-time pay when the operator runs 3+ shops. One new client at $400 (about PKR 110,000) pays for a full-time operator. Details in [plan/01](plan/01-hiring-tiktok-shop-operator.md#pay-be-honest-about-the-budget).
3. **No bots that fake being human on TikTok, Instagram or Facebook.** Anti-detect browsers, bulk account creation, private-API DM senders and scraping commenters to mass DM all break platform rules. We are a TikTok Shop agency: if our TikTok accounts or a client's shop get flagged, the business is over. Use the volume channels that are allowed (calls, cold email from separate domains, manual DMs, TikTok's own Affiliate Center for creators). See [plan/08](plan/08-automation-stack.md#what-we-will-not-automate-and-why).
