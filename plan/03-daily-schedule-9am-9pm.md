# 03. Daily Schedule (9 AM to 9 PM PKT)

The schedule follows our customers' clocks, not ours.

| Market | Their business hours | In Karachi (PKT) | What we do then |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UK / Ireland** (GMT in winter) | 9 AM to 5 PM | **2 PM to 10 PM** | Main cold-calling window, UK meetings |
| **EU** (CET) | 9 AM to 5 PM | 1 PM to 9 PM | Only English-speaking leads (e.g. Netherlands); not a focus for 19 days |
| **US East** (EST, since 1 Nov) | 9 AM to 5 PM | **7 PM to 3 AM** | US calls 7 to 9 PM; US meetings 7 to 11 PM |
| **US West** (PST) | 9 AM to 5 PM | 10 PM to 6 AM | Email only (async) |

So the **mornings in Karachi are for building**: lists, emails, follow-ups, training, delivery. The **afternoons and evenings are for talking**: calls and meetings.
