# 02. Founder Roles and Daily Tasks

**Rule 1: every founder sells every day.** Everyone dials, everyone has a number.
**Rule 2: no founder spends more than 45 minutes a day on client delivery after Day 5.** Delivery belongs to the operator. Founders review, they don't do.
**Rule 3: a reply on any channel stops every other channel for that lead.** Whoever got the reply owns the lead until it is booked.

Founder 4's name wasn't given, so this plan calls them **Founder 4**. Their role is designed around the gaps the other three leave: lead data, automation, a second calling shift, and project management.

## Hamza: Head of Outbound + Closer #1

**Outcome he owns:** booked meetings from cold email, and closed deals from his meetings.

**Daily tasks**
1. **9:20 to 9:50 AM: overnight replies.** Answer every reply that came in overnight from the US. Positive reply → send 2 time options + calendar link within 15 minutes of seeing it.
2. **Research 100 email leads a day** (Founder 4's tools help). Each lead needs: first name, verified email, brand, one specific detail (product, Amazon review count, "not on TikTok Shop").
3. **Load and run campaigns.** Check bounces (under 2%), spam complaints, inbox health every morning. Pause any inbox with problems.
4. **Write and test copy.** One A/B test at a time (subject line or first line), 200+ sends per version before judging.
5. **40 dials** in the UK block (2:00 to 3:00 PM).
6. **Run 1 to 2 closing calls a day** (6:15 PM to 9 PM slots).
7. **Same-day proposal + payment link** for every "yes" or "maybe yes".
8. Log numbers in the KPI tracker at 8:40 PM.

**Daily numbers:** 100 email leads added, 150 to 270 emails sent (as inboxes ramp), 100% of positive replies answered within 15 min during work hours, 40 dials, 1+ meeting booked, 1 to 2 meetings run.

## Zain: Closer #2 + Pipeline Owner

**Outcome he owns:** nobody falls through the cracks. Every lead that showed interest gets followed up until it is a yes, a no, or "contact in 30 days".

**Daily tasks**
1. **9:20 to 10:30 Follow-ups.** Work the pipeline: no-shows, "send me info", "call me next week", proposals not signed. **20 follow-ups a day**, each with a new angle (proof, question, deadline), never "just checking in".
2. **10 high-intent proposals a day:** Upwork jobs for "TikTok Shop manager/specialist", LinkedIn/Indeed posts where US/UK companies are *hiring* a TikTok Shop manager (pitch: "we cost less than one week of a full-time hire"), and Fiverr/Contra inbound.
3. **30 manual DMs/LinkedIn messages a day** to brand owners (personalised, no bots).
4. **Warm network and referrals:** Days 1 to 3, message every contact who knows someone selling online in the US/UK. Ask the first client for referrals.
5. **40 dials** in the UK block (3:00 to 4:00 PM).
6. **Run 1 to 2 closing calls a day.** Optional late shift on 3 days a week (meetings 9 to 11 PM PKT = 11 AM to 1 PM US Eastern).
7. **Meeting confirmations:** every booked meeting gets a calendar invite, a WhatsApp/email reminder the day before, and a message 1 hour before.

**Daily numbers:** 20 follow-ups, 10 proposals, 30 DMs, 40 dials, 1 to 2 meetings run, show rate above 70%.

## Huraiz: TikTok Shop Lead + Lead Caller

**Outcome he owns:** the most cold-call conversations on the team, and the "these people really know TikTok Shop" feeling on every closing call.

**Daily tasks**
1. **Days 1 to 3:** audit the first client's fulfillment against current TikTok Shop rules and fix it (see README, risk #1). Build the proof kit:
   - 1-page case study from his portfolio (real numbers only: GMV, orders, creators, videos, timeframe; screenshots with client names hidden unless they agree).
   - 3 to 5 screenshots of real results.
   - A 5-minute "TikTok Shop Opportunity Audit" template (screen recording: their product, 3 competitors selling on TikTok Shop with visible sold counts, 3 creator videos that sell similar products, our plan).
2. **Days 1 to 4:** technical interviews and test-task grading for the operator.
3. **Days 5 to 9:** train the operator (max 90 min/day, 10:00 to 11:30 AM).
4. **2:00 to 5:00 PM: UK power calling, 120 dials.** He is our best caller, so he gets the best calling window.
5. **Expert on closing calls:** joins Hamza's/Zain's calls for 10 minutes to answer "how will you actually do it?" questions. This raises the prospect's belief that it will work (Hormozi's "perceived likelihood").
6. **2 to 3 audit videos a day** for hot leads only (someone who replied positively or asked for more info). 15 minutes each.
7. **From Day 10: 5:00 PM QA, 30 minutes.** Check the operator's EOD report, spot-check listings and messages, review shop health.

**Daily numbers:** 120 dials, 10+ decision-maker conversations, 1+ meeting booked, 2 to 3 audit videos, expert on 1 to 2 calls, delivery time capped at 45 min after Day 5.

## Founder 4: Head of Lead Gen + Ops/PM

**Outcome he/she owns:** there is never a moment when someone has nobody to call. And the operator and clients are tracked so nothing slips.

**Daily tasks**
1. **9:20 to 11:00 Build tomorrow's call lists.** 300+ new verified call leads a day (UK + US), using Google Maps, Amazon brand research, Meta Ad Library and TikTok (see [04](04-icp-markets-lead-generation.md)). De-duplicate, score A/B/C, assign to each caller by name.
2. **Run the automation** on the GPU laptop and Claude Code (see [08](08-automation-stack.md)): Google Maps pulls, website checks (Shopify? TikTok Shop already?), lead qualification, personalisation lines for Hamza's emails.
3. **UK numbers:** screen UK leads against the TPS/CTPS lists before calling (see [05](05-offer-and-scripts.md#calling-rules-uk-and-us)).
4. **2:00 to 4:00 PM: UK calls (40 dials).**
5. **5:00 to 5:15 PM: ops check-in** with the operator (and Huraiz): orders, problems, client messages that need a founder.
6. **7:00 to 9:00 PM: US power calling, 60 to 80 dials** (9 to 11 AM US Eastern, the best time to reach US owners).
7. **Project manager:** keeps the project board and client deadlines current; sends client updates the operator prepares; runs the Monday client reports.
8. **8:40 PM: KPI review.** Collects everyone's numbers, shows the team the funnel, flags the weakest step.
9. **Days 1 to 4:** runs the operator hiring process (see [01](01-hiring-tiktok-shop-operator.md)).

**Daily numbers:** 300+ call leads + 50 email leads added, 100 dials total, KPI sheet complete by 9 PM, ops check-in done.
