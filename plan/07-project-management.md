# 07. Project Management: Deliver Without Stopping Sales

**Owner:** Founder 4 (project manager). **Quality:** Huraiz. **Doing the work:** the operator(s).

The problem we had: a founder got pulled into a client and sales stopped. The fix is three rules:

1. **Every task has one owner and a due date.** If it's not on the board, it doesn't exist.
2. **Founders review, operators do.** Max 45 minutes/day of delivery per founder after Day 5.
3. **Capacity before closing.** One part-time operator = 1 to 2 shops. One full-time operator = 3 to 4 shops. When client 3 signs, start hiring operator #2 from the bench (see [01](01-hiring-tiktok-shop-operator.md)).

---

## Tools (free)

- **Project board:** Trello, ClickUp or Notion free plan (or a Google Sheet tab). One board, one card per client, one checklist per stage.
- **Communication:** one internal WhatsApp group per client (founders + operator), plus the client's own group/email.
- **Files:** one Google Drive folder per client: `Assets / Listings / Reports / Access (no passwords) / Contract`.
- **Passwords:** password manager with sharing. Never passwords in WhatsApp.

## Board columns (each client card moves left to right)

`Signed → Onboarding → Setup → Listings → Live → Creators → Optimising`

| Stage | Tasks (checklist on the card) | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signed** | Payment received, agreement signed, onboarding form sent | Hamza/Zain | Day 0 |
| **Onboarding** | Kickoff call (30 min), Seller Center sub-account access, product list, stock location, **fulfilment method checked against TikTok rules**, brand assets | Huraiz (call) + Founder 4 (checklist) | Day 2 |
| **Setup** | Shop settings, shipping templates, return policy, warehouse address, MCF connector (if Amazon brand), shop health baseline | Operator, Huraiz reviews | Day 5 |
| **Listings** | Up to 20 listings: titles, images, attributes, prices, bundles for December | Operator, Huraiz approves first 5 | Day 10 |
| **Live** | All listings approved, test order, first creator invites | Operator | **Day 14 (our guarantee)** |
| **Creators** | 150+ invites a week, samples sent, videos tracked | Operator | Weekly |
| **Optimising** | Improve best sellers, pause weak listings, monthly strategy call | Operator + Huraiz | Monthly |

## Service levels (what we promise clients)

| Promise | Standard |
|---|---|

| Order processing | Every order within the shop's shipping deadline. **Zero late shipments.** |

## Daily rhythm for delivery

| Time (PKT) | What | Who |
|---|---|---|

| 5:00 to 5:30 | **QA:** spot-check 3 listings, 5 buyer replies, shop health per client | Huraiz |
| 9:00 PM | Operator 15-minute urgent check | Operator |

## Quality control checklist (Huraiz, daily 5 PM)

- [ ] No orders past their shipping deadline
- [ ] No open policy warnings or violations
- [ ] Shop health score not dropping
- [ ] 3 random listings checked: correct title, images, attributes, no banned claims
- [ ] 5 random buyer replies checked: polite, correct, on time
- [ ] Creator outreach on track for 150+/week
- [ ] Fulfilment still compliant (own stock, real supplier, or MCF; neutral packaging, no other retailer's invoices or branding)

## Client health score (Sunday)

Score each client green / yellow / red:

| | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shop health | No warnings | 1 warning, fixed | Repeated warnings or restriction |
| Delivery | All SLAs met | 1 miss | 2+ misses |
| Client mood | Happy, responsive | Quiet | Complaining, talking about cancelling |
| Results | Sales or videos growing | Flat | Falling |

**Any red:** Huraiz calls the client within 24 hours with a fix plan. Hamza or Zain joins if billing or scope is the issue.

## Employee performance (operator)

Reviewed every Monday against the KPIs in [01](01-hiring-tiktok-shop-operator.md#operator-kpis-reviewed-every-monday): late shipments, reply time, violations, creator invites, EOD report consistency. Two weeks of strong numbers → bonus. Two weeks of misses → written improvement plan with specific targets. A third week → replace from the bench.

## Our other services (AI receptionist, websites, marketing, lead gen)

Same system, same board, separate columns. During the 19-day sprint, **only take non-TikTok work if it doesn't need a founder to deliver it**, or if it's already signed. The sprint goal is TikTok Shop clients.
