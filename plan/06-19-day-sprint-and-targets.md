# 06. The 19-Day Sprint: Targets, Day-by-Day Plan, and Fixes

**Sprint:** Day 1 = Thu 5 Nov 2026 → Day 19 = Mon 23 Nov 2026.
**Target:** 4 new clients minimum (plan for 5 as a buffer). At $400 each that is **$1,600 to $2,000 new MRR**, plus the first client = **$2,000 to $2,400 MRR**.

---

## Work backwards from 4 clients (Hormozi: volume, not luck)

These are **starting assumptions**. Replace them with our own numbers after Day 3.

| Step | Assumed rate | Needed for 5 clients |
|---|---|---|
| Closed clients | | **5** |
| ← Meetings held | 15 to 20% close rate | **~30** |
| ← Meetings booked | ~70% show up | **~45** |
| ← Sources of meetings | calls ~30, email ~10, Upwork/referrals/DMs ~5 | |
| ← Call conversations with decision-makers | ~10% book a meeting | **~300** |
| ← Dials | ~8% reach a decision-maker | **~3,700** over 13 weekdays ≈ **300/day** |
| ← Cold emails | ~0.5 to 1% positive reply, half book | **~3,000** |

**Conclusion:** 300 dials a day, 200+ emails a day once inboxes are ready, 60 DMs and 10 Upwork/job-post proposals a day. That is the price of 4 clients in 19 days.

## Find the broken step (Hormozi: component, not category)

Check these every evening. Fix the **first** step that is below target, not everything at once.

| Number | Target | If it's low, it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision-maker reach (conversations ÷ dials) | 8%+ | Bad numbers or wrong time | Better lists (owner names from Companies House), call 9 to 11 AM their time |
| Meeting rate (meetings ÷ conversations) | 10%+ | Opener or offer is weak | Change the reason line, lead with the free audit, try a new niche |
| Email positive replies | 0.5 to 1%+ | List or first line is weak | Better signals in line 1, tighter niche |
| Email bounces | under 2% | Bad data | Verify every list, pause inbox if above 3% |
| Show rate | 70%+ | Weak confirmations | Reminder day before + 1 hour before, ask "which products sell best?" at booking |
| Close rate (deals ÷ meetings held) | 15%+ | Low belief or high risk | Huraiz on every call, stronger proof, guarantee, payment link ready on the call |
| Proposal to paid | 60%+ | Slow follow-up | Payment link in the call; follow up same night and next day |

## Weekly review (Sunday 6 PM)

60 minutes, all four founders. Founder 4 runs it.

1. **Scoreboard** (10 min): every weekly target vs actual.
2. **Wins and losses** (10 min): each closed and each lost deal, and why.
3. **Broken step** (15 min): pick the single weakest number. Agree one change.
4. **Listen to 2 calls** (10 min): one good, one bad.
5. **Delivery check** (10 min): every client's shop health, deadlines, happiness (Huraiz + Founder 4).
6. **Next week** (5 min): targets per founder, leads per caller, campaigns to launch.
