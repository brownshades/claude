# 01. Hire the TikTok Shop Operator (4-day process)

**Owner:** Founder 4 runs the process. Huraiz is the technical interviewer and trainer. Hamza/Zain stay on sales.
**Deadline:** Offer accepted by **Sun 8 Nov**, operator starts **Mon 9 Nov**.

Why this matters: every hour a founder spends processing orders is an hour of sales lost. The operator is not a "nice to have". It is the thing that lets four founders sell full time.

---

## Pay: be honest about the budget

- **PKR 10,000 to 15,000 is below the legal minimum for a full-time job in Sindh.** The minimum wage is PKR 40,000/month for unskilled workers (FY 2025-26; one source reports PKR 43,000 from July 2026). The hourly minimum was PKR 192/hour in 2025.
- At PKR 15,000 you can legally and fairly buy about **3 to 4 hours a day, 6 days a week, part-time**. That is enough for 1 to 2 small shops.
- **Recommended offer (fits the budget now, grows with us):**
  - **Base: PKR 15,000/month, part-time (about 4 hours a day, 6 days a week), remote or hybrid.**
  - **+ PKR 5,000/month for each extra shop they run** (shop 2, shop 3...).
  - **Written step-up:** when they run 3+ shops, move to full-time at **PKR 40,000 to 50,000/month**.
  - Small performance bonus each month for zero late shipments and zero policy violations (for example PKR 2,000).
- Why this is cheap for us: one new client at $400 is roughly PKR 110,000 a month (at about PKR 280 per USD). Four new clients is about PKR 450,000 a month. Paying a good operator well protects all of that revenue. "At any cost" should apply here first.
- If the best candidate asks for PKR 20,000 to 25,000 part-time, **pay it**. A bad operator costs us clients.

## Who we are looking for

**Must have**
- Has done real work in at least one of: TikTok Shop Seller Center, Amazon Seller Central (VA work), Daraz seller panel, Shopify store operations.
- Writes clear, polite English (they will answer US and UK buyers and creators).
- Organised, careful with details, reports honestly when something goes wrong.
- Own laptop, stable internet **plus a backup** (mobile data), and **UPS or other backup power** (load-shedding cannot mean late shipments).
- Available every day in a fixed window (for example 10 AM to 2 PM PKT, plus a 15-minute evening check).

**Nice to have**
- TikTok Affiliate Center (target collaborations, free samples).
- Amazon MCF / FBA, CJ Dropshipping or other supplier dashboards.
- Canva and CapCut (simple product images and video edits).
- Finished a course such as Extreme Commerce, Enablers, or DigiSkills e-commerce.

**Not a fit:** people who only did "TikTok automation" investor schemes, people who cannot show any real work, people with no power/internet backup.

## 15-minute screening call questions

1. Walk me through a TikTok Shop (or Amazon/Daraz) order from "Awaiting shipment" to "Delivered". What can go wrong?
2. A buyer says the item arrived damaged and wants a refund. What do you do, step by step?
3. What makes a good product title? Give me one for a "stainless steel water bottle, 1 litre, keeps drinks cold 24 hours".
4. How would you find TikTok creators for a skincare product, and what would you send them?
5. The shop gets a policy warning. What is the first thing you do?
6. What hours can you work every day? What happens when there is load-shedding?
7. Tell me about a mistake you made at work and how you fixed it.

**Green flags:** specific answers, mentions of tracking, timelines, screenshots/evidence, "I would tell my manager immediately". **Red flags:** vague answers, blaming others, no backup plan for power/internet.

## Paid test task (send at 10 AM Sat, due 2 PM)

1. **Listing:** write a full TikTok Shop listing for a product we pick (title, 5 bullet points, description, key attributes, list of the 5 images you would need).
2. **Customer messages:** write replies to these three buyers:
   - "My order is 5 days late, where is it?"
   - "I got the wrong size. I want to return it."
   - "This is a scam, I want my money back now." (item is actually in transit)
3. **Creators:** find 15 TikTok creators who would fit the product. For each: handle, follower count, why they fit, average views on recent videos. Then write the invite message you would send.
4. **Order problem:** "10 orders are due to ship today but the supplier says stock arrives in 3 days." What do you do and who do you tell?

## Account security (do this before giving any access)

- Give access through **Seller Center user/sub-account permissions** with only the roles needed (orders, products, customer service, affiliate). **No access to finance or payouts.**
- Never share the client's main login or 2FA device. The client (or the agency's shared admin account) keeps 2FA.
- Use a password manager with sharing (Bitwarden free plan works) for any other tools.
- Give the operator an agency email (not their personal Gmail) for tools.
- Sign a 1-page agreement: role, hours, pay, confidentiality, no contacting clients directly about payments, 7-day notice.

## Operator daily SOP (part-time, about 4 hours)

Times in PKT. Adjust to the shop's shipping deadlines.

| Time | Task | Done when |
|------|------|-----------|
| 10:00 | **Orders:** process every order in "Awaiting shipment"; buy labels or pass to fulfillment; upload tracking. Check the shop's shipping deadline (recent guidance for US orders: in transit within about 2 business days). | Zero orders past deadline |
| 10:45 | **Customer messages:** reply to every message. Target under 12 hours. | Inbox at zero |
| 11:15 | **Returns, refunds, cancellations:** handle the queue, escalate anything over $50 or any "scam/chargeback" message to Founder 4 | Queue at zero |
| 11:45 | **Shop health:** check violations, account health score, late shipment rate, cancellation rate, low stock | Any issue posted in the team group |
| 12:00 | **Listings:** create or improve 3 to 5 listings (only if the shop needs them) | Listings live, no policy flags |
| 12:45 | **Creators:** send 30 to 50 targeted invites in Affiliate Center, reply to creators, approve samples, chase posted videos | Numbers logged |
| 1:45 | **End-of-day report** in the client's WhatsApp group (internal) and the tracker | Report sent (template in [templates/operator-eod-report.md](../templates/operator-eod-report.md)) |
| 9:00 PM | **15-minute check:** new urgent messages, US morning orders | Nothing urgent left |

## Operator KPIs (reviewed every Monday)

- Late shipments: **0**
- Customer reply time: **under 12 hours**, response rate 95%+
- Policy violations: **0**
- Creator invites sent per shop per week: **150+**
- Samples sent and videos posted (tracked weekly)
- EOD report sent: **every working day**

When the operator runs 3 shops well for 2 weeks, move them to full-time and hire operator #2 from the bench.
