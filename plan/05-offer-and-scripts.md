# 05. The Offer and Every Script

Built with the frameworks in `rediumvex/hormozi-sales-cold-outreach-claude` (Value Equation, guarantees, MAGIC naming, lead-magnet opener, CLOSER-style closing) and the cold-email / sales-enablement rules in `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` (short, peer tone, one ask, no AI tells).

**Golden rule: only use real numbers.** Every `[REAL RESULT]` below must come from Huraiz's actual portfolio. Never invent sales figures or "your competitors do $50k/month" claims. Made-up claims kill trust the moment a prospect checks.

---

## The offer (Hormozi Value Equation)

`Value = (Dream outcome × Likelihood it works) ÷ (Time delay × Effort for them)`

| Lever | How our offer pushes it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | "Your products selling on TikTok Shop, with creators making videos for you, in time for the December gifting rush." |
| Likelihood | Huraiz's real results, a free audit video of *their* niche, and a guarantee |
| Time delay | Shop live in **14 days** from getting access and products |
| Effort | **We do everything:** setup, listings, creators, orders, customer service. They approve and ship (or MCF ships). |

### Name (MAGIC formula)
**"The 30-Day TikTok Shop Launch for [Amazon brands / UK boutiques / skincare brands]"**

### What they get (value stack)
1. TikTok Shop setup or fix-up (account, shipping, policies, shop health)
2. Up to 20 product listings written and optimised
3. Creator program: **150+ targeted creator invites a week** through TikTok's Affiliate Center, plus free-sample management
4. Daily order processing and customer service (replies within 12 hours)
5. Weekly WhatsApp/email report, monthly strategy call
6. **Bonus:** holiday gift bundles listed for December
7. **Bonus:** if they sell on Amazon, we connect FBA stock to TikTok via Amazon MCF

### Price
- **$400/month (UK: £299/month), month-to-month, 14-day notice to cancel.** First month paid up front.
- Present it as a **founding-client rate** (normal rate $750). **Real scarcity:** "We take 5 founding clients this month because each shop gets a dedicated operator." This is true: our operator capacity is limited.

### Guarantee (conditional, based on things we control)
> "Your shop will be live with your listings within 14 days of us getting access and your products, and we'll invite at least 150 creators a week. If we miss either one, your second month is free."

Never guarantee sales numbers. Guarantee the work.

### Lead magnet (Hormozi "give first")
**Free "TikTok Shop Opportunity Audit":** a 5-minute screen-recording video by Huraiz showing (1) their product, (2) 3 similar products selling on TikTok Shop with visible sold counts, (3) 3 creator videos already selling that type of product, (4) what we would do in the first 30 days. **Only for hot leads** (positive reply, or asked for more info), max 3 a day.

## Calling rules (UK and US)

- **UK:** before calling, screen numbers against the **TPS** (individuals and sole traders) and **CTPS** (companies) registers. Don't make live marketing calls to registered numbers unless they have agreed to hear from us. Always give our agency name and a way to opt out.
- **US:** call business numbers by hand. **No autodialers or recorded messages to mobile numbers.** Call between 9 AM and 5 PM in *their* time zone (the legal window is 8 AM to 9 PM). If anyone says "don't call", mark them `do_not_contact` immediately.
- **Recording:** if you record calls, say so at the start ("this call may be recorded for training"). Some US states require everyone's consent.
- Get UK and US local numbers on a VoIP dialer (for example Quo/OpenPhone, JustCall or Zadarma). Local numbers get answered far more than +92.

## Cold call script (main, all tiers)

Speak slowly, smile, simple words. The goal of the call is **a 25-minute meeting**, not a sale.

**1. Opener (permission)**
> "Hi, is this [Name]? Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from [Agency]. You weren't expecting my call. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can tell me if it's worth a chat?"

**2. Reason (one sentence, about them)**
> "We help [boutiques / Amazon brands / skincare brands] in the [UK/US] sell on **TikTok Shop**. We set up the shop, list your products, and get TikTok creators making videos about them. Are you selling on TikTok Shop at the moment?"

**3a. If NO:**
> "Got it. Most [boutiques] we speak to say the same. Is that because you haven't had time, or you tried it and it didn't work?"
> *(Listen. Let them talk.)*
> "That makes sense. What I'd suggest is a 25-minute call with our TikTok Shop lead, Huraiz. He'll show you 3 shops in your exact niche and what they sell. If it's not a fit, no problem. Would [day] at [time] or [time] your time work better?"

**3b. If YES, but slow:**
> "Nice. How's it going? Are creators posting about your products, or is it mostly you?"
> *(Listen for the problem: no sales, no creators, no time, account issues.)*
> "That's the exact thing we fix. Can I book you 25 minutes with Huraiz? He'll look at your shop before the call. [day] at [time] or [time]?"

**4. Lock it in**
> "Perfect. What's the best email for the invite? I'll also send a WhatsApp reminder. And so the call is useful, which 2 or 3 products sell best for you right now?"

**Gatekeeper**
> "Hi, I'm hoping you can help me. Who looks after your online sales, like the website and social media?" → "Great, what's the best way to reach [Name]? Is there a direct email?"

**Voicemail (15 seconds, only to business lines)**
> "Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from [Agency]. I help [boutiques] like yours start selling on TikTok Shop. I'll send you a short email with an idea for [Business]. My number is [number]. Thanks."

**Urdu opener for Pakistani/South Asian-owned businesses (A3)**
> "Assalam o alaikum, [Name] sahab/sahiba? Main [Your name] bol raha hoon [Agency] se, Karachi se. Aap ka 30 second le sakta hoon? Hum UK mein [clothing boutiques] ko **TikTok Shop** par sell karwaate hain. Shop setup, listings, aur TikTok creators se videos banwaana, sab hum karte hain. Kya aap abhi TikTok Shop par sell kar rahe hain?"
> *(Then switch to English or stay in Urdu, whatever the owner prefers.)*

### Cold call objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Not interested." | "Fair enough. Is that because sales are good right now, or TikTok just isn't a priority?" *(One loop only. If still no: thank them, mark `nurture_30d`.)* |
| "Send me an email." | "Sure. So I send something useful, would you rather see your competitors on TikTok Shop, or what it costs?" → get the email → "I'll send it now. Let's pencil 25 minutes on [day] so you can ask questions. [time] or [time]?" |
| "How much is it?" | "It's $400 a month, no long contract. But first let's see if TikTok Shop even makes sense for your products. That's what the 25 minutes is for." |
| "We tried TikTok, didn't work." | "That's common. Usually it's no creators or weak listings. What did you try?" *(Listen.)* "Huraiz can show you what we'd change. [time] or [time]?" |
| "We do it ourselves." | "Great. How many creators are posting about your products each week?" *(If low:)* "That's the part we take off your plate." |
| "Is TikTok even safe? Wasn't it getting banned?" | "That's settled. TikTok's US business moved into a new US joint venture in January 2026, and TikTok Shop keeps growing." *(UK: "TikTok Shop has been running in the UK since 2021.")* |
| "I'm busy." | "Totally get it. Is later today or tomorrow morning better for 2 minutes?" |

### After booking (Zain's confirmation sequence)
1. **Right away:** calendar invite + email: "Looking forward to [day]. Huraiz will look at [product] on TikTok Shop before we talk."
2. **Day before:** WhatsApp/text: "Hi [Name], quick reminder of our call tomorrow at [time] your time. Here's the link: [link]."
3. **1 hour before:** "See you in an hour, [Name]."
4. **No-show:** within 10 minutes: "Hi [Name], we missed you. No problem. Does [time] tomorrow or [time] work?"

## Cold email

### Setup (Hamza, Days 1 to 3)
- **Never send from our main domain.** Buy 3 lookalike domains (e.g. `get[agency].com`, `[agency]hq.com`), 2 to 3 inboxes each (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365), SPF + DKIM + DMARC set up.
- New inboxes need warming. **Fastest option ("at any cost"):** buy **pre-warmed inboxes** from a cold-email infrastructure provider so we can send 20 to 25 per inbox per day from Day 3. Otherwise ramp: 10/inbox/day (Days 1 to 4), 20 (Days 5 to 8), 30 (Day 9+).
- Use a sending tool with warm-up and reply detection (Instantly or Smartlead), or our Claude Code + Composio setup **only with the Workspace inboxes on secondary domains**.
- **Verify every email** before upload. Keep bounces under 2%.
- **Turn off open and click tracking.** Plain text. No images, one link at most.
- Every email has an opt-out line and our postal address. UK: email limited companies' work addresses; don't cold email sole traders or partnerships without consent.
- Run the `email-deliverability-audit` and `spam-word-checker` skills (coldoutboundskills) before every new campaign.

## The closing call (25 minutes, CLOSER structure)

Run by Hamza or Zain. Huraiz joins for the "how" part. Have the payment link and onboarding form ready **before** the call.

| Step | Minutes | What to say / ask |
|---|---|---|
| **C**larify why they're here | 2 | "Thanks for making time. Before I share anything, what made you take this call?" |
| **L**abel the problem | 3 | "So if I heard you right, you want [their goal], and the problem is [their words]. Is that right?" |
| **O**verview past pain | 5 | "What have you tried so far? How did that go? What happens if nothing changes by January?" |
| **S**ell the vacation (outcome) | 7 | Huraiz shares the screen: 3 shops in their niche selling, what we'd do in the first 30 days, their shop live in 14 days, creators posting, orders handled. Talk about the result, not the tasks. |
| **E**xplain away concerns | 5 | "What would stop you from starting this week?" Handle with the table below. |
| **R**einforce the decision | 3 | "Here's what happens next: you pay the first month today, fill a 10-minute onboarding form, and we start setup tomorrow. Shall I send the link?" |

**The close line:**
> "We have 2 founding-client spots left this month at $400. The guarantee means your risk is one month at most. Want to grab one?"

### Closing objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "It's expensive." | "Compared to what? A part-time TikTok Shop hire in the US costs far more per week. If the shop sells [X] orders a month, it pays for itself. And the guarantee covers you." |
| "I need to think about it." | "Of course. Usually that means one of three things: the money, the timing, or whether it will work for you. Which one is it?" (Then handle that one.) |
| "Let me talk to my partner." | "Makes sense. Can we book 15 minutes tomorrow with both of you, so they can ask Huraiz directly?" |
| "Can you do it cheaper?" | "The price is already our founding rate. What I can do is [add one bonus, e.g. 5 extra listings]. Price stays the same." |
| "I'm worried about TikTok rules / getting banned." | "Good. That's why Huraiz checks every listing and fulfilment step against TikTok's rules. Shop health is part of our weekly report." |
| "Can I start in January?" | "You can, but December is the biggest gifting month. If we start now, creators are posting before the rush. If you start in January, you miss it." |

### After a "yes" (same evening)
1. Send payment link (Payoneer/bank transfer; have it ready) and a 1-page service agreement.
2. Send the onboarding form: products, stock location, fulfilment method, brand assets, Seller Center access (sub-account), goals.
3. Book a 30-minute kickoff with Huraiz within 48 hours.
4. Founder 4 creates the client on the project board (see [07](07-project-management.md)).

### After a "not yet"
Zain owns it. Follow up on Day 2 (send the audit video or a relevant example), Day 5 (new proof), Day 10 (deadline: "founding rate ends on [date]"). Then `nurture_30d`.
